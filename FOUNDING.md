# Founding History

## The Beginning of NITM-SOSC

The NIT Manipur Students' Open Source Community (NITM-SOSC) was initiated to create a collaborative environment where students can move beyond classroom learning and participate in building meaningful, real-world open-source technology.

Founded by **Wahengbam Heramani Singh**, NITM-SOSC began with the vision of bringing together students from different academic departments and years to learn independently, build collaboratively and contribute to the global open-source ecosystem.

The community aims to provide a platform for student-led projects, technical leadership, knowledge sharing and meaningful contributions to open source.

## Our Continuing Vision

NITM-SOSC is intended to grow beyond its founding members. Its long-term direction will be shaped through collaboration, transparent governance, responsible project maintenance and the contributions of future generations of student developers.
