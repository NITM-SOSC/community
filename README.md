# NITM-SOSC

### NIT Manipur Students' Open Source Community

**Learn Independently. Build Collaboratively. Contribute Globally.**

An independent, student-led community initiated by students of the National Institute of Technology Manipur.

We bring together curious minds who believe in learning beyond boundaries, building meaningful software and contributing to the global open-source ecosystem.

---

### / About

NITM-SOSC is a space for students to explore technology independently, exchange knowledge, collaborate on software and contribute to open-source projects.

We believe that meaningful engineering comes from curiosity, experimentation and collaboration — not just learning about technology, but actively building with it.

### / What We Do

| Initiative | Focus |
|:--|:--|
| **Explore** | Discover technologies, tools and emerging fields. |
| **Learn** | Exchange knowledge and learn independently. |
| **Contribute** | Participate in real-world open-source projects. |
| **Build** | Develop and maintain collaborative software. |
| **Share** | Document discoveries and make knowledge accessible. |

### / Areas of Interest

`Artificial Intelligence` · `Cybersecurity` · `Software Engineering` · `Linux` · `DevOps` · `Cloud Computing` · `Systems Programming` · `Emerging Technologies`

Our interests are not limited to any particular language, framework or discipline.

### / Who Can Participate

Initially, NITM-SOSC welcomes students from all departments and academic years of NIT Manipur.

No prior open-source experience is required.

Whether you're exploring your first programming language, experimenting with Linux, developing applications or contributing to established projects, there's room to learn and participate.

**Curiosity matters more than experience.**

### / Get Involved

- Explore our repositories.
- Participate in community discussions.
- Share technical discoveries and resources.
- Find projects and collaboration opportunities.
- Contribute to existing open-source software.
- Build and maintain projects with fellow students.

Start with our [Contributing Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md).

### / Principles

**Openness** — Share knowledge, ideas and software.

**Independence** — Encourage self-directed learning and experimentation.

**Collaboration** — Learn from one another and build together.

**Inclusivity** — Welcome curiosity across disciplines and experience levels.

**Sustainability** — Create projects that outlast individual contributions.

### / Beyond NIT Manipur

Our initial community is rooted in NIT Manipur.

Our long-term vision is to cultivate a sustainable open-source culture and eventually connect aspiring student developers across colleges and universities throughout Manipur.

We want to contribute to an ecosystem where students don't just consume technology — they build, maintain and improve it.
