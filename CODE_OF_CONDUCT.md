# Code of Conduct

## NITM-SOSC — Community Standards

### Building an open, respectful and collaborative community.

NITM-SOSC is committed to creating an inclusive environment where students can learn, experiment, collaborate and contribute to open-source software without fear of harassment, discrimination or disrespect.

We believe that great technology is built through diverse perspectives, mutual respect and shared knowledge.

This Code of Conduct applies to all members, contributors, maintainers and participants in NITM-SOSC activities, including GitHub repositories, discussions, events and other community spaces.

---

## 01 / Our Principles

Every member of NITM-SOSC is expected to uphold the following principles.

**Respect Everyone**

Treat everyone with dignity and respect, regardless of their experience, academic background, department or technical expertise.

**Encourage Learning**

Welcome beginners and support people who are exploring unfamiliar technologies. Nobody should be discouraged from asking questions or learning something new.

**Collaborate Constructively**

Share knowledge, exchange ideas and provide meaningful feedback. Disagreements should focus on technical decisions rather than personal attacks.

**Respect Contributions**

Recognise the work of others, respect intellectual property and give appropriate attribution.

**Take Responsibility**

Be accountable for your actions, communicate honestly and contribute towards maintaining a healthy community.

---

## 02 / Expected Behaviour

We expect all community participants to:

- Communicate respectfully and professionally.
- Welcome contributors regardless of their experience level.
- Provide constructive and actionable feedback.
- Respect different opinions and technical approaches.
- Give credit to original authors and contributors.
- Respect project maintainers and their responsibilities.
- Accept criticism and feedback professionally.
- Help create a supportive learning environment.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Follow applicable project licences and contribution requirements.

---

## 03 / Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable within NITM-SOSC:

**Harassment and Discrimination**

- Personal attacks, threats or intimidation.
- Discriminatory comments or behaviour.
- Unwanted harassment or persistent unwanted contact.
- Deliberate humiliation or targeted hostility.

**Abusive Communication**

- Insults, offensive language or degrading remarks.
- Trolling or deliberately disruptive behaviour.
- Repeatedly targeting individuals during discussions.
- Publishing private information without consent.

**Misuse of Community Resources**

- Spam, malicious links or deceptive activities.
- Impersonating other members or maintainers.
- Misrepresenting someone else's work as your own.
- Deliberately disrupting projects or community activities.
- Sharing confidential project or member information without authorisation.

**Abuse of Authority**

- Using maintainer privileges to intimidate or unfairly disadvantage contributors.
- Misusing administrative permissions.
- Retaliating against individuals who report misconduct in good faith.

---

## 04 / Technical Discussions and Disagreements

Technical disagreements are a normal part of software development.

Members are encouraged to challenge ideas, question implementations and suggest alternative approaches.

However:

- Criticise implementations, not individuals.
- Support technical arguments with reasoning or evidence.
- Respect decisions made through established project governance.
- Avoid turning technical disagreements into personal conflicts.
- Accept that different solutions may be valid in different contexts.

We value thoughtful disagreement and constructive collaboration.

---

## 05 / Responsibilities of Maintainers

Community maintainers and project leaders have additional responsibilities.

They are expected to:

- Lead by example.
- Apply community standards consistently.
- Encourage participation from newcomers.
- Review contributions fairly and constructively.
- Avoid abusing administrative privileges.
- Handle reports of misconduct responsibly.
- Maintain appropriate confidentiality during investigations.
- Communicate moderation decisions clearly where appropriate.

Leadership within NITM-SOSC is a responsibility, not a privilege to exercise authority unfairly.

---

## 06 / Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the NITM-SOSC community maintainers.

**Reporting channels:**

- Contact the community maintainers privately through the organisation's official contact email.
- Alternatively, contact an appropriate community administrator privately.

Please avoid reporting sensitive incidents through public GitHub Issues or Discussions.

When submitting a report, include:

- A description of the incident.
- Relevant dates and context.
- Links or screenshots, if available.
- The individuals involved, where known.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible.

We will not tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith.

---

## 07 / Enforcement

Community maintainers are responsible for addressing violations of this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the nature and severity of the incident, possible actions include:

1. Informal guidance or a warning.
2. A formal warning.
3. Temporary restriction from community discussions or activities.
4. Temporary suspension of contribution privileges.
5. Removal from community spaces.
6. Permanent removal from the community in serious or repeated cases.

Enforcement decisions should consider the context, severity, available evidence and history of previous violations.

Where practical, affected individuals should have an opportunity to provide relevant context.

Serious incidents may require immediate protective action while a review is conducted.

---

## 08 / Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- NITM-SOSC GitHub organisation and repositories.
- GitHub Issues, Pull Requests and Discussions.
- Community meetings and events.
- Online learning and collaboration spaces.
- Official communication channels.
- Any other space where an individual represents NITM-SOSC.

Community members are expected to uphold these standards whenever participating in activities associated with NITM-SOSC.

---

## 09 / Continuous Improvement

A healthy community requires continuous effort.

This Code of Conduct may be reviewed and updated as NITM-SOSC grows, introduces new activities and welcomes new contributors.

Members are encouraged to suggest improvements through the appropriate community governance process.

---

## / Our Commitment

We want NITM-SOSC to be a place where curiosity is encouraged, knowledge is shared and people feel comfortable building together.

You do not need to be the most experienced developer in the room.

You only need the willingness to learn, contribute and respect others.
