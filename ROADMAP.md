# Membership

## NITM-SOSC — Become Part of the Community

### A community built on curiosity, independent learning and open collaboration.

NITM-SOSC (NIT Manipur Students' Open Source Community) is an independent, student-led initiative that brings together students interested in exploring technology, building software and contributing to the global open-source ecosystem.

We believe that meaningful participation should be driven by curiosity and willingness to contribute, not by prior experience or technical qualifications.

Membership is an opportunity to learn independently, collaborate with others and become part of a growing community of student developers.

---

## 01 / Who Can Join?

During our founding phase, membership is open to students of the **National Institute of Technology Manipur**, irrespective of:

- Department or academic discipline.
- Academic year or semester.
- Programming experience.
- Technical specialisation.
- Previous open-source contributions.

Whether you are writing your first program, exploring artificial intelligence, experimenting with Linux, studying cybersecurity or developing software, you are welcome.

**You don't need to be an expert to belong here.**

### Future Expansion

NITM-SOSC begins at NIT Manipur, but our long-term vision extends beyond a single campus.

As the community matures, we aspire to connect with aspiring developers and technology enthusiasts from other colleges and universities across Manipur.

Any expansion of membership eligibility will be introduced through a transparent community decision and an updated membership policy.

---

## 02 / Our Membership Philosophy

We believe membership should encourage participation rather than create unnecessary barriers.

Our approach is based on four principles:

### Open Participation

Members should have opportunities to explore projects, share ideas and participate in technical discussions.

### Independent Learning

Everyone is encouraged to develop skills at their own pace, explore unfamiliar technologies and pursue their interests.

### Collaborative Development

Members can collaborate on projects, review code, exchange knowledge and learn from one another.

### Shared Responsibility

A sustainable community depends on members who respect its values, contribute constructively and help maintain a welcoming environment.

---

## 03 / How to Join

Joining NITM-SOSC should be straightforward.

**Step 1 — Explore the Community**

Visit our GitHub organisation:

https://github.com/NITM-SOSC

Explore our repositories, documentation and community discussions.

**Step 2 — Introduce Yourself**

Visit our [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/orgs/NITM-SOSC/discussions) and introduce yourself.

You can share:

- Your name or preferred username.
- Your department and academic year.
- Technologies or subjects that interest you.
- What you are currently learning or building.
- Areas where you would like to collaborate.

Only share personal information you are comfortable making available to the community.

**Step 3 — Participate**

Start exploring projects, asking questions, sharing resources and joining discussions.

**Step 4 — Get Involved**

Find an issue, contribute to a repository, propose a project or collaborate with other members.

Your participation is what makes the community meaningful.

## 04 / Membership and Participation

NITM-SOSC recognises different forms of participation.

These are participation roles, not separate classes of people.

| Participation | Description |
|---|---|
| Community Participant | Explores discussions, resources and community activities |
| Contributor | Contributes to repositories, documentation, research or projects |
| Project Maintainer | Takes responsibility for maintaining a particular project |
| Community Maintainer | Helps coordinate community operations and governance |

Members may participate in multiple ways depending on their interests and availability.

Taking on a maintenance role requires additional responsibilities and appropriate permissions.

GitHub organisation membership and repository access are managed separately from general community participation.

---

## 05 / Member Expectations

We do not expect every member to contribute code regularly or participate in every community activity.

However, members are expected to:

- Respect fellow members and contributors.
- Follow the community Code of Conduct.
- Participate constructively.
- Respect intellectual property and software licences.
- Avoid plagiarism and misrepresentation.
- Protect confidential information and credentials.
- Respect project maintainers and their responsibilities.
- Communicate responsibly when collaborating.
- Avoid misuse of community resources.

We encourage members to contribute whenever they can.

There is no expectation that every member must be active at all times.

---

## 06 / Projects and Collaboration

Members are encouraged to initiate and participate in open-source projects.

You can:

- Propose a new software project.
- Join an existing project.
- Form a collaborative development team.
- Contribute to established open-source repositories.
- Explore emerging technologies.
- Share research and technical experiments.
- Maintain projects that you have helped build.

Projects should have clearly documented goals, responsibilities and contribution guidelines.

Project ownership and maintenance should follow the community's governance and repository policies.

---

## 07 / Membership Fees and Selection

NITM-SOSC is an independent, student-led learning and development community.

Membership is not based on programming tests, competitive selection or prior development experience.

There are no mandatory membership fees under this founding membership policy.

Any future financial requirements or changes to membership arrangements must be communicated transparently and approved through the appropriate community governance process.

---

## 08 / Membership Conduct

Every member is expected to follow:

- [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)
- [Contribution Guidelines](CONTRIBUTING.md)
- [Community Governance](GOVERNANCE.md)

Violations may result in appropriate action under the applicable community policies.

Members are encouraged to raise concerns through the designated community reporting channels.

---

## 09 / Inactivity and Membership Changes

We recognise that students have academic responsibilities, personal commitments and changing availability.

Temporary inactivity does not automatically mean someone has lost their place in the community.

However:

- Members may voluntarily leave the community.
- Organisation access may be reviewed when necessary.
- Access may be restricted for security or policy violations.
- Project maintainers should communicate when they can no longer maintain their responsibilities.

Any removal or restriction should follow applicable governance procedures.

---

## 10 / Independence and Institutional Identity

NITM-SOSC is an independent, student-led community.

Its activities, projects, governance and membership are organised by participating students.

The use of the NIT Manipur name identifies the community's origin and initial student focus. It does not, by itself, imply official institutional recognition, sponsorship or endorsement.

Any future institutional affiliation or formal recognition will be communicated accurately and transparently.

---

## 11 / Our Long-Term Vision

We are beginning at NIT Manipur with a simple objective: create an environment where students can independently learn, collaboratively build and contribute to open-source software.

Over time, we aim to:

- Develop a sustainable open-source culture.
- Build and maintain meaningful software projects.
- Encourage contributions to established global projects.
- Connect aspiring developers across Manipur.
- Create opportunities for peer learning and technical collaboration.
- Establish a community that continues growing across graduating batches.

We want NITM-SOSC to become more than a collection of GitHub repositories.

We want it to become a community of people who build, share and contribute.
